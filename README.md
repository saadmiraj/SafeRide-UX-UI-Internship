# SafeRide-UX-UI-Internship
A safety-first ride-sharing mobile app designed to empower women with real-time safety features. UI/UX case study covering research, wireframes high-fidelity design, accessibilty and developer handoff. 
SafeRide 
Ride with confidence. Get home safe.
# About
SafeRide is a safety-first ride-sharing mobile app concept designed to give women more control, visibility, and peace of mind during every trip. This repository documents the complete 4-week UI/UX design sprint from research to developer handoff.
# What We Did - 4 Week Breakdown
Week 1: Research & Strategy
We started by understanding the problem.  
Conducted user interviews and surveys to identify safety pain points for women in ride-sharing.  
Competitive analysis of Uber, Careem, and safety apps  Created user personas, user journeys, and defined the core problem statement.  
Mapped the Information Architecture and primary user flows: Onboarding → Booking → Ride → SOS

Week 2: Wireframing & Flow
Focused on structure before visuals.  
Sketched low-fidelity wireframes for all key screens.
Designed the full user flow with special focus on the Emergency SOS journey.  
Conducted usability testing on wireframes and iterated based on feedback.  
Finalized the app structure and navigation.

Week 3: Visual Design & Prototyping
Brought the app to life.  
Built a design system: color palette, typography, iconography, and reusable components.  
Designed high-fidelity screens for onboarding, home/map, booking, ride tracking, profile, and emergency.  
Created an interactive Figma prototype of the full safety journey.  Internal team review and design refinements.

Week 4: Polish, Accessibility & Handoff
Made it dev-ready and presentation-ready.  
Full UI audit for consistency in spacing, colors, and components.  
Accessibility pass for color contrast and 44px tap targets as per WCAG.  
Developer handoff: Figma Dev Mode, design tokens, component specs, and handoff documentation.  
Branding assets: App icon, 1-pager intro, and mini brand guidelines.  
2-3 min walkthrough video of the prototype.  
Final demo presentaion.
# Key Features
One-Tap SOS - Instantly alerts emergency contacts with live location + audio.  
Trusted Contacts - Auto share trip details and live tracking.  
Driver Verification - Safety score, ratings, and ID check.
Safe Routes - Avoid isolated/low-light areas, especially at night.  
In-App Safety Toolkit - Fake call, share ride, and quick exit.
